# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging. Opened earbuds and headphones cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless faulty.

## How to return
Email support@gadgethub.example with your order number. We reply within one business day with a prepaid return label. Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

## Faulty items
Faulty items are replaced or refunded at no cost, including return shipping. All products carry a 12-month warranty from the delivery date.

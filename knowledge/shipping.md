# Shipping

## Delivery times and costs
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $4.99. Orders over $75 ship free. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $12.99.

## Order tracking
A tracking link is emailed once your order ships, usually within 24 hours of payment. Orders placed after 3 PM ship the next business day.

## International shipping
We currently ship only within the United States and Canada.

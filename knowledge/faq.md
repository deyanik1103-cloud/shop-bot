# Frequently Asked Questions

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Apple Pay. Payment is taken when the order is placed.

## Changing or cancelling an order
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of placing them. After that they enter packing and cannot be modified.

## Contact and support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern, at support@gadgethub.example.

## Warranty
Every product has a 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage and water damage are not covered.
